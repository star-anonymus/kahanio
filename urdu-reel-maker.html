How do I make an Urdu reel for free?

Open Kahanio, write your script in Urdu, or in Hindi letters if that is easier, and choose an Urdu AI voice such as Uzma or Asad. Pick a template and press Make my video. You get a 9:16 MP4 with Urdu captions in Nastaliq, music and real footage, made in your browser with no sign-up.

Make a free video now

Step by step

  1. Type your topic, then write the script in the script box: one scene per line.
  2. In step 3 choose AI voice, then Urdu, then Uzma (female) or Asad (male). Press Listen to hear the first line.
  3. Pick a template. Clean and Cartoon read well with Urdu text.
  4. Leave Real video footage on for stock clips behind each scene, or add your own photos.
  5. Press Make my video, then download the MP4 and the captions file.

Can I write in Hindi letters and still get an Urdu voice?

Yes. Choose Hindi script, Urdu voice in step 3. Your captions stay in Hindi letters as you wrote them, and Kahanio turns each line into Urdu letters before the Urdu voice speaks it. Spoken Hindi and Urdu are close, so the voice sounds Urdu. Roman Urdu (Urdu in English letters) is not read well by the Urdu voices, so use Urdu or Hindi letters.

Common mistakes

  1. Long sentences: keep each scene to one or two short sentences so the captions stay readable.
  2. Writing in Roman Urdu for the AI voice: use Urdu or Hindi letters instead.
  3. Posting the same template video again and again: add your own voice or opinion so the video is clearly yours.
