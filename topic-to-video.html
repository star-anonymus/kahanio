Topic to video: turn any topic into a short video

Updated 17 November 2026

How do I turn a topic into a video?

Type the topic in Kahanio. Write the script yourself, use the quick starter, or let a free AI write it with your own free key. Choose a template, a voice and the format, then press Make my video. Kahanio adds captions, music and stock footage and gives you an MP4, made in your browser.

Make a free video now

Three ways to get the script

  1. Write it yourself: one scene per line. Your own words make the most original videos.
  2. Quick starter: a five-scene draft from your topic, with no AI, for you to edit.
  3. Free AI: Groq, Google Gemini or OpenRouter's free models, with your own free API key. The key stays in your browser tab.

An example

Topic: cold email tips. Script: "Most cold emails get no reply. Here is why." / "Start with the client's problem, not your service." / "Keep it under five sentences." / "End with one easy question." With the Story template, an AI voice and real footage on, Kahanio makes a vertical video of about 20 seconds with each word highlighted as it is spoken.

Common mistakes

  1. Too many scenes: four to six short scenes keep a reel under 45 seconds.
  2. No search words: add words in [brackets] at the end of a line, like [laptop, office], to get better footage.
  3. Not checking an AI script: always read and fix it before you post.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Kahanio works without sign-up. Videos are made in your browser and are not uploaded to us.

How long can the video be?

It follows your script. About 20 to 45 seconds suits reels and Shorts.

Where does the footage come from?

From Pexels' free stock library. Each video credits the filmmakers on its end card, and you get credit text for your description.

Can I add my own photos?

Yes. Add photos in step 2 and they move with slow, smooth 3D camera motion.
