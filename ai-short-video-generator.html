Will a generated video hurt my YouTube channel?

YouTube's monetisation rules leave out inauthentic content: mass-produced, repetitive videos with no personal touch. Use the tool to save time, then add your own voice, opinion or footage, and turn on YouTube's altered or synthetic content setting when a video could look real.

Common mistakes

  1. Removing the footage credits: keep them in your video description.
  2. Using the same script for many videos: change the words and the angle each time.
  3. Skipping the AI-assisted label when the platform asks for it.
