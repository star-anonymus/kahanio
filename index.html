    What is Kahanio?

    Kahanio is a free AI reel maker. You type a topic or a script, and it makes a short video with word-by-word captions, an AI voice in English, Urdu or Hindi, music made for each video and real stock footage, as a 9:16 or 16:9 MP4. It runs in your browser with no sign-up.

    1. Write your script, use the quick starter, or let a free AI write it.
    2. Pick a template, a voice and the format.
    3. Download the MP4 and the captions file, ready to post.

    Questions

    Is Kahanio really free?

    Yes. Kahanio is free with no sign-up and no watermark over your video. A short end card at the end credits the footage and the tool.

    Which languages can the AI voice speak?

    English, Urdu (Uzma, Asad) and Hindi (Swara, Madhur). You can also write in Hindi letters and have an Urdu voice speak it, or record your own voice.

    Are my videos uploaded?

    No. The video is made in your browser. Only the text of a scene is sent to make an online AI voice, and stock footage is fetched from Pexels.

    Can I use the videos on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok?

    Yes. Keep the footage credits in your description, add your own voice or opinion so the video is clearly yours, and use the platform's AI label when it asks for one.

    Post it the right way

    Add yourself

    YouTube's rules leave out mass-produced, repetitive videos from monetisation. Your own voice, opinion or footage makes the difference.

    Label AI honestly

    Turn on the platform's "altered or synthetic content" setting when a video could look real.

    Keep the credits

    Pexels and Pixabay clips are free to use. Keep the credits in your description, and never sell the clips on their own.
