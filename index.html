    Before you post on YouTube

    • YouTube's monetisation rules leave out "inauthentic" content: mass-produced, repetitive videos with no personal touch. Add your own voice, your own opinion or your own footage.
    • Turn on YouTube's "altered or synthetic content" setting when a video could look real.
    • Keep the footage credits in the description. Pexels and Pixabay clips are free to use, but never sell them on their own.
